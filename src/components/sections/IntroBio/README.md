# Intro Bio

The home page bio shares the header's editorial composition through a first-line indent aligned with the independent logo. The logo stays in the header; the em dash belongs to the bio and is included with its text animation. The intro measures its rendered lines after the header entrance completes, then reveals and fades those lines after scrolling begins. It remains hidden while scrolling back up and replays its line reveal only at the top.

Line groups are rebuilt when the paragraph width changes. Reduced-motion preferences skip the entrance and scroll animations. The component only mounts on the home route.
